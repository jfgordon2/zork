## UI and placements

The current-source host harness uses the actual button handlers, interpreter, renderer, packed RGB555 scenes and sprite headers, with a memory-copy video backend. It exercises focus, Nearby, bag, full scrolled action lists, argument pickers, directions, cancellation and unread story pages. Browsing does not change turns, score or location. Full-screen dial/speech interfaces intentionally replace the scene; that is not counted as an art-occlusion failure.

These are repeated entries and checks, not unique puzzles. Natural compass probes and targeted optional/time-state branches extend the captured room inventory to **71 room IDs / 74 room-light states**. Additional state captures cover changing encounters, doors, floor contents and outcomes. The CSV contains **232 captured-state rows**, plus **18 uncaptured catalog-room rows**. It records evidence and distinguishes baseline menu coverage from state-specific checks.

The normal story/menu drawer starts below the 240×76 art band. No progression-blocking UI access failure was found in the sampled paths. The cliff marker issue is a genuine header overlap. Invisible/unmapped props and the omitted crowded staff remain selectable through focus and Nearby; that preserves access but does not satisfy visual placement QA. The year chooser's help and controls fit at native size.

The crowded-drop sweep tests **268 floor-item selections in 48 states**. It records **158 absent-marker instances**, mostly repetitions of missing item poses and Royal Puzzle omissions, plus the crowded museum staff case. These are visual omissions, not evidence of lost objects. No portable selection rectangle crossed the art boundary in that sweep. Separate actor checks found the cliff overlap. Hood/cloak selections on the still-dressed actor are not counted as missing character art merely because they lack separate rectangles.

The following current-state checks pass:

- Lantern floor drop, leave/return, Save/Restore and launcher Continue, including matching art crops.
- Time-machine pushes across museum, entrance and Jewel Room, then Restore to its original room and crop.
- Concealed ring, real time travel, recovery from under the seat, and Restore to the hidden state.

- **Seven accepted museum floor drops** surviving leave/return and Restore, with the saved scene crop reproduced.
- Royal Puzzle logical position and walls restored correctly; visual cache limitation is Z3-13.

Native mGBA uses the byte-identical cartridge and real GBA button inputs. Starting SRAM fixtures are loaded to isolate scenarios; the Lua scripts read state for assertions and do not move objects or edit the VM. The checked sequences include lamp persistence, Royal Puzzle push/Restore/resurvey, sailor Hail/gift/take, potion passage past all three Guardian views, cliff chest/rope/rescue, hood mercy/Restore, three-digit year selection and past Jewel Room, remote Master orders and the ending, underwater object pickup, beam interruption/Restore, and taking the visually omitted crowded staff. Preview images are native 240×160 captures enlarged without smoothing; the full scene board uses the production host renderer.

Controller replay resolves **201 exact / 53 equivalent / 0 missing** main-route commands and **206 exact / 55 equivalent / 0 missing** Guardian-route commands. The main replay omits three redundant failures and substitutes four waits; the Guardian replay substitutes one wait. Failed actions and hazards were additionally exercised in the targeted scripts above, but not every failure was entered through buttons.

`make all test` passes the existing three-story suite. Scratch source-derived harnesses, inputs, full story transcripts, saves and native capture scripts are in `build/zork-iii-audit/`. Harness assumptions were corrected during the audit: full-screen dial pickers need different visibility expectations, and the native compact-enum puzzle-map layout differs from the host layout. The underwater pickup probe was also replayed through its actual More actions → Use → Take path. The corrected checks passed. All eleven native batches completed.

## Coverage limits

This is a broad visual, branch, controller, UI and persistence audit, not an exhaustive proof of every command, random combat outcome, time period, maze position or item arrangement. The room-object catalog contains 89 IDs; some are temporal variants or scenes that resolve entirely inside narration. The per-screen CSV marks uncaptured room IDs as **Unchecked**, including past museum variants, several other mirror-side positions, Ladder Top/Bottom, Machine/Drafty rooms, another prison variant and the alternate Royal Puzzle Entrance. The sacrificial altar and brief Ladder Bottom transition were observed in text, without a stable interactive scene capture.

Use the [scenery rules](../../assets/SCENERY_RULES.md) for the follow-up pass. Prioritize puzzle landmarks and missing items, then connected scenery and state variants, then presentation polish. Preserve the working Guardian identities, grounded encounter poses, original hazards and story-owned persistent state while addressing the listed issues.
