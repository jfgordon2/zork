# Zork I audit

This is the baseline audit. The rebuilt cartridge and its verification are documented in the [repeat audit](zork-i-re-audit.md).

Audited Release 119, serial 880429, in `zork-trilogy.gba`. The complete run visited 83 room IDs and entered the Stone Barrow with 350 out of 350 points and the Master Adventurer rank. The route replay checked the commands exposed by the GBA menus and compared equivalent commands against the bundled story. It resolved 334 exact and 106 equivalent inputs. Five redundant failed inputs replayed as Look to preserve turn timing.

I compared native 240 × 160 renders of the visited rooms against the story text. Branch replays covered additional rooms and states. In mGBA I used the GBA object menu to open the Living Room trap door and the GBA keyboard to reproduce the Dam Base error. The screenshots include both emulator captures and frames from the cartridge renderer. [Audit screenshots](docs/images/zork-i-audit-preview.png)

## Gameplay checks

| Route or puzzle | Observed behavior |
| --- | --- |
| White House | The boarded front door refused to open. The leaflet was inaccessible until the mailbox opened and disappeared from the box when read and taken. Entering the kitchen window before opening it failed. Opening it allowed entry. |
| Cellar and maze | The rug exposed the trap door. The descent barred it behind the player. A lit lantern supported travel to the Troll Room. Going onward in darkness killed the player by grue and triggered the story's recovery. |
| Forest grating | Disturbing the leaves exposed the locked grate. Opening or attempting to pass it failed while closed. The skeleton key unlocked it from below. The open shortcut worked in both directions. |
| Troll and thief | The armed troll kept his axe out of reach and blocked the west exit. Sword attacks knocked him unconscious and then killed him. The axe remained after the body disappeared. Giving the thief the egg, recovering the opened egg and canary, and recovering treasures all worked along the full route. The thief could also kill the player on entering his hideaway. |
| Cyclops | The awake Cyclops blocked the stairs. Lunch followed by water put him to sleep and let the player go upstairs. Saying Ulysses drove him away and opened the Strange Passage shortcut to the Living Room, which worked in both directions. |
| Dam and reservoir | The wrench failed before the controls were enabled. A knife failed on the enabled bolt. The wrench opened and closed the sluice gates. The drained reservoir exposed the trunk. The blue maintenance button flooded the room and staying there drowned the player. |
| Hades | The spirits blocked passage. Reading the book first or ringing the bell alone failed to clear the gate. Bell, lit candles, and prayer in sequence drove them away and opened access to the skull. |
| Mine and bat | Carrying garlic prevented the bat from grabbing the player. Entering without it sent the player to Ladder Bottom. The brass lantern allowed travel through the Gas Room. Bringing the lit torch triggered the gas explosion and the story's death sequence. The coal-to-diamond route worked. |
| River and falls | The pump inflated the boat. Launching from the boat, moving downstream, taking the buoy, and landing on Sandy Beach worked. Staying in the current too long carried the player over the falls. The immaterial rainbow refused crossing. Waving the sceptre made both crossings work. |
| Mirrors, egg, and ending | Rubbing a mirror moved the player to the counterpart room. Opening the egg by hand failed. Dropping it from the tree damaged the egg and canary. The full treasure route opened the Barrow and completed the chapter. |

The strongest visual matches were the introductory house views, the kitchen tabletop, the troll on the chamber floor, the bat on the ceiling, the Cyclops changing pose, the wraiths clearing the gate, and the chalice in the thief's room. Darkness also removed the room art and objects as expected. Story paragraphs occupy their own panel below the art. The focus badge, compass, and action drawer occupy parts of the picture.

## Follow-up fixes

1. **P1 · Keep `SWIM` at Dam Base inside the story.** With the boat still folded, use **Start → Enter command** and enter `swim`. The cartridge displays “Object id #0 referenced” at story address 36744, opcode 170, and offers only a return to the trilogy. This reproduced in both the host replay and mGBA. Handle the missing object in this path so the player receives a story response and can continue.
2. **P1 · Keep focused items visible.** The focus badge covers 24 of the trident sprite's 66 visible pixels in Atlantis, hiding much of the shaft. The badge also crosses the mailbox post and the lower edges of some props. Move the badge or give it an alternate position based on the focused art's footprint. Check the compass and action drawer against the same footprints, especially when opening an object's menu on the right side of the scene.
3. **P2 · Show passable openings in the house.** In mGBA, opening the trap door produced the staircase description while the artwork still showed a shut hatch. The rear window rendered identically before and after opening. After the Cyclops opened the shortcut, the Living Room still showed the boarded west door. Add state-matched views of these openings and check them from both directions.
4. **P2 · Make the dam controls and water level readable.** The Dam artwork was pixel-identical with the sluices open and shut. The green indicator has a focus marker but no lit sprite. The Dam Lobby shows an underground lake, and the Maintenance Room shows a generic stone hall without its four colored buttons or rising floodwater. Link these views to the same story state as the puzzle and the draining reservoir.
5. **P2 · Show the boat and the changing riverbanks.** The same cave-lake backdrop covers the Dam Base and several Frigid River reaches. The boat stays invisible while folded, inflated, afloat, and beached. Sandy Beach becomes a masonry corridor. Give the river reaches continuous shoreline landmarks and place the boat, red buoy, and beach shovel where the text puts them.
6. **P2 · Stage the early treasure route.** Up a Tree reuses a ground-level forest view and omits the nest and visible egg. The Attic uses an underground hall without its table, rope, or knife. The maze skeleton's key and coins are also invisible. These are early objects that teach the player to look for visual clues.
7. **P2 · Give passage puzzles recognizable landmarks.** Both Mirror Rooms lack their mirrors. The Dome Room lacks the railing and drop, and the Shaft Room lacks the basket and chain. The grate changes state, but the clearing and the view from below have no connected opening geometry. Add the landmarks that explain the action and orient the player on the other side.
8. **P2 · Draw visible contents and ritual props.** Opening the coffin reveals the sceptre in the text while the drawn coffin looks empty. The torch is absent from its pedestal in the Torch Room. At Hades the dropped candles, red-hot bell, and ritual props are missing during the sequence. Place them on the described supports or ground and use the story's visibility and flame states.
9. **P2 · Split the Barrow approach from the ending.** The outside view of the massive stone tomb currently shows an underground corridor. The ending text describes entering a bright cavern with a stream and footbridge, while the game keeps the same interior plate. Give the approach and completion their own views.
