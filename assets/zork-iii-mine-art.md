## Pair acceptance

- The top and bottom use the same timber species/color, rail thickness, rung width and spacing rhythm, and rectangular opening construction.
- From the top the ladder visibly descends into the floor; from below the same ladder rises through the ceiling. Its projected location can shift with viewpoint, but the opening, rails, and direction must describe one continuous structure.
- Keep the ladder opening clear of the stair route and both rooms' walkable floors. The lower view retains readable west/south passages.
- Keep the shared rough mine stone and timber support style in the adjoining dead end without implying it is another ladder room.

These four 240×102 backgrounds were generated as one restrained 16-bit clustered-pixel set. For future edits, use both ladder images as references in the same edit and preserve the geometry above. User-facing placement notes and screenshots are maintained in the Zork III re-audit.
